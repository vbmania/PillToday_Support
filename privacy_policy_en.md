# PillToday Privacy Policy
Last updated: 2025-12-05

## 1. Data We Collect
- **Crash/diagnostic data**: Collected via Firebase Crashlytics (non-identifying crash logs: stack traces, device model, OS version, anonymized session/instance IDs).
- **User data**: Pill logs and reminder settings are stored only on-device (App Group) and are not transmitted externally.

## 2. Purpose of Use
- Improve app stability and investigate crashes (Firebase Crashlytics).

## 3. Storage and Transmission
- Pill logs/reminders: Stored locally on your device; not sent to servers or iCloud.
- Crash logs: May be sent to and stored on Firebase Crashlytics servers (no personal identifiers).

## 4. Third Parties and Ads
- No ads, IDFA, or profiling.
- No third-party analytics/trackers other than Crashlytics.

## 5. Sync/Backup
- iCloud sync is not provided; each device works independently.

## 6. Data Deletion / Opt-out
- Deleting the app removes local data.
- If you don’t want crash collection, uninstalling the app stops collection.

## 7. Security
- Local data uses the App Group sandbox.
- Network transmission (Crashlytics) is over HTTPS.

## 8. Contact
- Please open a GitHub Issue in this repository. Include device model, OS/app version, and reproduction steps if possible for faster support.
