# DupCleaner Support
Last updated: November 12, 2026

## About the App
- DupCleaner helps you review duplicate files in selected folders and duplicate photos and videos in your authorized photo library on iPhone and iPad.
- File and photo cleanup use the same purchase entitlement. Review your choices before the final cleanup confirmation.
- File and photo comparisons run on-device. Checking iCloud originals, purchases, analytics, diagnostics, and available advertising features may use the network.

## How to get help
- **Email**: [contact.aiworkerslab@gmail.com](mailto:contact.aiworkerslab@gmail.com)
- **GitHub Issues**: You may also report general problems without personal data in this repository. Issues are public.
- Include when possible for faster resolution:
  - Device type, OS version, and App version
  - Whether the problem involves files or photos
  - Storage location type, such as local storage, iCloud Drive, or an external drive, or your photo access scope
  - Steps taken, expected result, and actual result
- You do not need to send personal files or photos, account passwords, payment details, or raw logs. If you attach a screenshot, hide personal information such as file names, paths, and photos.

## FAQ
- **Are similar photos duplicates?** The photo feature checks originals for exact duplicates. A candidate is not yet a confirmed duplicate, and the App does not recommend the best shot among similar photos.
- **Why does checking iCloud items take time?** Originals may need downloading when unavailable locally. Review the App’s original-check and network guidance and its unfinished items. Completion time depends on your connection and the external provider.
- **Will I lose my selections when I close the App?** Starting with 1.6.2, file and photo review results and selections are saved locally for reopening. This does not resume initial file enumeration or hashing at the exact interruption point. Restoration alone does not automatically download or delete anything.
- **What can the App access?** File access covers folders chosen with the system picker. Photo access covers the full or limited library you authorize. You can change photo access in system settings.
- **Can I disable analytics and diagnostics?** You can turn usage analytics and diagnostic sharing off separately in the App’s settings. See the privacy policy for provider processing and data already transmitted.
- **Can I recover deleted photos?** Recovery follows the Photos app’s Recently Deleted feature and Apple’s retention conditions. iCloud Photos deletions may sync to other devices using the same account. DupCleaner does not provide separate recovery copies. File Trash support depends on the storage location and provider.
- **Does uninstalling delete originals?** Uninstalling removes the App’s local data, but original files and photos are separate. Uninstalling is not cleanup of originals or deletion of data already sent to external services.
